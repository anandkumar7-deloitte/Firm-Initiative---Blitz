# Approval matrix email (new panelist referrals)

**When to use:** someone wants to refer a new panelist for a skill, and you need to tell them
whose sign-off is required before the referral can be added to MasterPanel.

**Before sending:** confirm your organization's actual current approval matrix — the rows below
are the Blitz team's standard tiers, but double-check the specific "who approves whom" chain is
still accurate before quoting it, and confirm any skill-specific extra-approver requirements (see
the note below) are current.

---

Hi,

*(This email is being sent via BCC to protect recipient privacy.)*

Please find below the approval matrix for panel confirmation requests:

| Role | Approval Required From |
|---|---|
| Senior Manager | Managing Director |
| Manager | Senior Manager |
| Senior Consultant | Manager and Senior Manager |
| Consultant | Senior Manager and Managing Director |

<<If a skill needs an extra named approver:>>
For **<<skill list>>**, we additionally require sign-off from **<<Name>>** (**<<email>>**) —
please obtain their approval and share it with us alongside the above.

Thank you for your support.

For any clarifications, please reach out to the <<Team>> Blitz team.

Regards,
Blitz Team

---

### Companion: asking someone how to refer a new panelist

> Hi <<Name>>,
>
> To refer a new panelist for <<Skill(s)>>, we'd need approval from their Manager, Senior
> Manager, or Managing Director (based on the panelist's own designation — see the matrix above).
>
> Could you share the email address of the person you'd like to refer? I'll send over a sample
> approval email you can forward.
