# Travel booking reminder (in-person events)

**When to use:** a finalized panelist needs to travel from a non-event city, and hasn't
confirmed/completed their booking by the internal deadline.

**Before sending:** personalize per person — don't send a templated placeholder version, fill in
each person's actual name and home location.

---

Hi **<<Name>>**,

Following up urgently on your travel booking for the **<<Event name>> — <<date>>, <<city>>
(In-Person)**.

You were finalized as a panelist based on your survey response, and we initiated the booking
process to arrange your travel from **<<Location>>**. We haven't heard back from you yet, and our
deadline to close this was **<<deadline date>>**<<" — we're now a day past that" if overdue>>.

Could you please confirm your travel booking status **<<today / by a specific time>>**?

If there's any issue on your end preventing you from attending, please get approval from
**<<approval authority, e.g. L65>>** for a drop-out, and kindly help us identify a backup
panelist.

Appreciate a quick response — thank you!

Regards,
Blitz Team

---

### Notes from real usage
- A soft "as soon as possible" tends to get slower responses than a concrete cutoff — use a
  specific time if you're already past deadline.
- If a contradiction comes up later (e.g. someone who said they're dropping later claims they're
  "already on the panel"), don't assume either way — ask a direct clarifying question that forces
  a clear yes/no, and check whether anyone else (another coordinator) has already opened a
  separate thread with them on the same ask, so you're not duplicating effort.
